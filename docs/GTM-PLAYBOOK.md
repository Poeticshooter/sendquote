**Message template:**

**Post cadence:** 3x/week
**Content pillars:**
- Pain point posts: "Still sending PDF quotes in 2026? Here's what it's costing you."
- Behind-the-build: "I built an AI quoting tool as a solo founder. Here's what I learned."
- Value posts: "3 questions to ask before sending a quote (that double your close rate)"

> You don't know:
> • If they opened it
> • What they looked at
> • If they shared it
> • When to follow up
>

>
> You draft in 10 seconds (AI helps). They get a beautiful page. You get real-time notifications.
>
> Free to try: sendquote.in
>
> Built by a solo founder in India 🇮🇳

### Channel 3: Community Engagement (Days 1-14)
- **Reddit:** r/IndiaBusiness, r/Entrepreneur, r/SaaS — answer questions, don't pitch
- **Indie Hackers:** Share your build story, ask for feedback
- **Telegram groups:** Indian freelancer/agency groups
- **Product Hunt:** Launch after you have 5-10 users for social proof

## 5. PRODUCT HUNT LAUNCH CHECKLIST

### Pre-Launch (7 days before)
- [ ] Get 5-10 real users (use the outreach playbook above)
- [ ] Collect 2-3 testimonials (even if informal)
- [ ] Prepare launch post: "I built SendQuote as a solo founder because PDF quoting is broken"
- [ ] Create Product Hunt listing with screenshots + GIF
- [ ] Tell your network: "I'm launching on PH on {date}, would appreciate support"

### Launch Day
- [ ] Post on LinkedIn, Twitter, Reddit, Indie Hackers
- [ ] Message everyone who said they'd support
- [ ] Respond to every comment within 30 minutes
- [ ] Update the listing with any feedback

### Post-Launch
- [ ] Follow up with everyone who upvoted
- [ ] Write a "lessons from launching" blog post
- [ ] Track conversion: PH visits → signups → quotes created

2. Day 1: "Create your first quote in 10 seconds" email
3. Day 3: "See how your quote performs" (tracking/analytics)
4. Day 7: "Upgrade to send more quotes" (if applicable)
